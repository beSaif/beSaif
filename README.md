# Hi there! 👋

I'm [Saif](https://codesaif.dev), a software engineer and MSc Computer Science student at the University of Geneva. I build mobile and web products end to end, and lately LLM agents and the tooling around them.

I like getting things into people's hands: since 2022 I've shipped 12+ products. At FeatsClub I ran the Stripe payments for 8+ organisations (over $150K) and shipped 25+ releases to 1,500+ users.

I also do a lot of hackathons. At sprintd Zurich my team took 3rd place in the Equans challenge with a tool that prices a 200-page Swiss German tender. Before that, I built a humanitarian negotiation trainer with the @ICRC team at Swiss {ai} Weeks, a hiking safety assistant for the HPE & @NVIDIA agentic AI challenge, and a bus reallocation model for TPG.

Outside of that, I build small web apps at [codesaif.dev](https://codesaif.dev), lift weights and make pixel-art side projects.

I'm also slowly mapping every drinking fountain in Geneva for an app.
